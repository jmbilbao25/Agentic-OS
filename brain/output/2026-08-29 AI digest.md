## Patterns
- Skills plus progressive disclosure plus cache-stable prompts is the same recipe showing up in four independent projects (ratel, dao-code, TaoLive harness, CaSKG); this is becoming the de facto shape of an agent OS and your hybrid-search second brain should adopt the "graph of skills behind a disclosure layer" model rather than treating notes as flat chunks.
- "Context engineering" is consolidating into a small set of mechanical primitives — compaction, prefix stability, BM25-plus-semantic retrieval, skill graphs — and away from prompt-craft; if your routines still spend tokens on raw history, you are paying for a problem the rest of the field has already engineered around.
- The capture is missing anything on evaluation, failure logging, or human-in-the-loop review for personal agents, which is conspicuous given that is exactly where a personal OS will silently degrade without it.
