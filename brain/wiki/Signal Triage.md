# Signal Triage

A capture of 87 items contains about two that matter. Triage is the discipline of
finding those two and **discarding the rest without guilt**.

The failure mode is not missing something. It is keeping everything — which produces
a vault that is expensive to search, full of material nobody read, and
indistinguishable from a feed.

## The one question

> Does this change a decision I am going to make?

Yes → distil into `brain/wiki/` in your own words, now. Maybe → leave it in
`brain/raw/`, it is in git. No → discard.

Anything that fails this but feels interesting is entertainment. Nothing wrong with
that; it does not belong in a second brain.

## Output is graded, never flat

| Grade | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Worth your time** | changes a decision, or invalidates a belief |
| **Worth knowing about** | you should recognise the name in six months |
| **Pattern** | true of the capture as a whole, not of any single item |
| **Skip** | dominated the capture, deserves nothing |

**Pattern is the grade that justifies the exercise.** No individual item contains
it. Observed on the first real radar run: skills-plus-progressive-disclosure-plus-
cache-stable-prefixes appearing independently in four unrelated projects in one
week. That is worth more than any single link in the capture, and a flat summary
would never surface it.

## Absence is a finding

Ask what the capture does *not* contain that it should. The first digest this repo
produced noted that nothing in the capture addressed evaluation, failure logging, or
human-in-the-loop review — "exactly where a personal OS will silently degrade". That
observation was more actionable than any paper in the capture, and it led directly
to [[Evals Before Vibes]].

A conspicuous gap means either the field has not solved it, or you are reading the
wrong sources. Both are worth knowing.

## Counter-signals

Low value, regardless of how it is presented:

- a benchmark number on a benchmark you do not use
- stars with no recent commits
- a launch post — a company existing is not a technical development
- novelty that is only scale
- a wrapper around something you already call directly

High value, regardless of how boring:

- names a failure mode you have hit or will hit
- reports a **negative** result, or a limitation the field usually hides
- shows a mechanism instead of a score
- contradicts something you currently believe
- arrives independently from three unrelated directions in one week

That last pair is why hype-sorted feeds are actively misleading: the strongest signal
available is *boring, well-engineered, unglamorous work*, and popularity ranking
systematically buries it.

## Anti-hoarding

A `brain/raw/` capture unread for two weeks was not important. Delete it. Every kept
file is a tax on every future search, and the instinct to keep things "just in case"
is precisely how a vault becomes a landfill.
