# Triage

A capture of 87 items contains, on a good day, two that matter. Triage is deciding
which two — and the discipline is that **most things get discarded**.

## The one question

> Does this change a decision I am going to make?

- **Yes** → distil it into `brain/wiki/` now, in your own words, with the source
  linked.
- **Maybe later** → leave it in `brain/raw/`. It is in git. Do not promote it.
- **No** → discard. This is the correct outcome for most items and it should feel
  fine.

Anything that fails this question but feels interesting is entertainment. There is
nothing wrong with that; it just does not belong in a second brain.

## Grades

Output is graded, never flat. A summary treating 87 items as equally important has
done no work.

| Grade | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **Worth your time** | changes a decision, or invalidates something you believe | read the primary source today |
| **Worth knowing about** | you should recognise the name in six months | one line, no reading |
| **Pattern** | true of the capture as a whole, not of any one item | this is where the real value is |
| **Skip** | dominated the capture, deserves no attention | say so, and say why |

The **Pattern** grade is the one that justifies the exercise. "Four independent
projects converged on skills-plus-progressive-disclosure" is worth more than any
single item in the capture, and no individual item contains it.

## Signals of low value

- A paper whose contribution is a benchmark number on a benchmark you do not use
- A repo with stars and no recent commits
- A launch post — a company existing is not a technical development
- Anything whose novelty is scale ("but bigger")
- A framework that wraps a thing you already call directly
- Anything you cannot restate in one sentence after reading the abstract

## Signals of high value

- Names a failure mode you have hit or will hit
- Reports a negative result, or a limitation the field usually hides
- Shows a *mechanism*, not a score
- Contradicts something you currently believe
- Appears independently from three unrelated directions in the same week
- Is boring, well-engineered, and unglamorous — this is the strongest signal there
  is, and the one hype-driven feeds systematically bury

## Reporting the gap

Ask what the capture does **not** contain that it should. Conspicuous absence is a
finding: it usually means either the field has not solved it, or you are reading the
wrong sources. Both are actionable, and neither shows up if you only summarise what
is present.

## Anti-hoarding

If a `brain/raw/` capture has sat unread for two weeks, it was not important.
Delete it. The instinct to keep everything "just in case" is what turns a vault
into a landfill, and the cost is paid on every future search.
